# The Frontier command design

The command experience keeps the existing original Canvas renderer and deterministic simulation. Interaction research informed the decisions below; no referenced code, artwork, interface layout or game asset was imported. This is a source review, not a claim of hands-on testing of every reference game.

## Input contract

On touch, tap a friendly unit, then open ground to move or an enemy to attack. A selected worker directed at a valid source gathers through the existing travel/carry/delivery economy. Mixed selections keep useful worker context commands restricted to workers. Empty-ground desktop left-click still deselects; right-click issues contextual orders.

An immediate drag pans. A stationary touch arms selection after 320 ms and displays a gold cue; dragging then displays a rectangle. Once a drag becomes a pan, waiting cannot turn it into selection. Area offers immediate explicit selection. Another finger cancels selection and enables camera gestures; cancellation, blur and resizing clear incomplete gestures. These decisions occur outside simulation state.

Browser probes measure delivered pointer timestamps, rather than treating a requested automation delay as elapsed touch time. The retained threshold distinguishes observed quick pans from cued held selection in the tested environments. This is emulated input verification; physical-device and broader human usability testing remain necessary.

## Context and tactics

Stable Area and Deselect controls sit outside the changing action strip. Workers see economy/construction actions; soldiers see movement, attack, hold, assault, retreat and stop; producers show their own recruits, queue and rallies. Global Build and Recruit tabs retain convenient settlement management. Camera controls remain independent of military orders.

Coordinated Assault uses a shared strategic route and short regroup/staging phases. Real units continue to move and fight through ordinary terrain, range, cooldown and damage rules. Retreat sends units to reachable spaced destinations at their normal speed, suppressing pursuit until arrival or a new order. Neither command grants resources, healing, teleportation, invulnerability or a combat-stat advantage.
