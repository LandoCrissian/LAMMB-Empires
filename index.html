Your first expedition

Select workers and tap trees, stone, gold or a farm. They gather, carry and deliver supplies to your Town Center.

Desktop
Click to select · drag a selection box · Shift adds units · right-click to move, gather or attack. WASD/arrows pan, wheel zooms. V selects workers, G selects army, H centers home, J holds position, Space pauses.
Touch
Tap to select. Choose Move or Attack, then tap the field. Drag to pan; use two fingers to pan and pinch. Choose a building, tap a location, then Place building.
