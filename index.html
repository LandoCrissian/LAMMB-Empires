FIELD GUIDE

Your first expedition

Select workers and tap trees, stone, gold or a farm. They gather, carry and deliver supplies to your Town Center.

Build a barracks to recruit swordsmen and archers. Towers cover your approach. Farms provide finite food; build more as they run out.

Touch: tap a friendly unit, then open ground to move. Tap an enemy to attack or a resource to gather. Hold until the gold cue appears, then drag to select a group. Area offers the same selection without holding; × deselects. A quick drag pans, and two fingers pan and zoom.

Assault advances a mixed army together with a brief regroup near defenses. Retreat sends survivors to the ground you choose at their normal speed. They remain vulnerable, and new orders take effect immediately.

Select a completed Town Center, Barracks or Siege Workshop to set a rally point. Tap a destination, then Confirm Rally. Town Center workers can rally to a deposit or completed farm and gather normally. Clear Rally restores idle production exits.

The Siege Workshop trains slow rams that hit structures hard. Escort them with soldiers: ordinary infantry can quickly dismantle an exposed ram.

Desktop
Click to select · drag a selection box · Shift adds units · right-click to move, gather or attack. WASD/arrows pan, wheel zooms. V selects workers, G selects army, H centers home, J holds position, K prepares an assault, R prepares retreat, Escape cancels or deselects, and Space pauses.
Touch
Tap a unit, then open ground to move or an enemy to attack. Hold for the gold cue and drag to select; Area also selects without holding. Drag immediately to pan; use two fingers to pan and pinch. Choose a building, tap a location, then Place building.

The Ember settlement lies northeast. Raids arrive along the roads. Protect your workers and keep archers behind your swordsmen. Hold Position keeps soldiers in place and fires only within weapon range. Stop cancels orders and restores normal defensive pursuit. Armies form loose ranks with archers behind the front line.
